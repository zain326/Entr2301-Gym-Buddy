How it works

  1. Build your profile. Share your goals, experience and weekly availability in about two minutes.
  2. Meet your matches. See compatible MRU students with clear reasons why you fit.
  3. Connect safely. Send an interest, meet in public campus spaces, and decide together.

Why students use it

  • Build friendships with classmates who share your interests.
  • Make workouts easier to stick with by finding a consistent partner.
  • Feel more connected to the MRU community.

Expected benefits, not guaranteed outcomes.

Your comfort comes first

Everything is voluntary. You choose who you connect with, where you meet, and you can decline or remove any connection at any time. MRU Gym Buddy is a fitness and community service, not a counselling or medical service.

Safety & support

Meet in public

Always plan first workouts in busy public campus spaces during reasonable hours. Tell a friend where you are going.

Trust your instincts

You can decline any connection, end a workout early, or remove a connection at any time. No explanation is required.

Keep personal details private

Never share passwords, financial information or your schedule outside the app. MRU Gym Buddy will never ask for your student ID or passwords.

Reporting and blocking

In the live service, every profile will have report and block tools reviewed by the student team. These controls are not active in this prototype.

Not a medical service

MRU Gym Buddy supports fitness and community. It is not counselling, therapy or medical care. MRU students can access support through MRU Student Wellness services.

Your data

This prototype stores preferences only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. A future version will use secure, consent-based data handling.

MVP Test Plan

How we plan to test whether MRU students want a workout partner matching service. This page describes a proposed plan. No testing has been completed yet and no results are shown here.

Based on our Value Proposition Canvas and workbook analysis, we prioritized workout partner matching to address social connection and motivation. We selected a free, voluntary matching prototype to test student interest before investing in a complete application.

1 Riskiest Assumption

MRU students are willing to use a free, voluntary platform to find compatible workout partners based on fitness goals, schedules, and interests.

2 Target Testers

10 Mount Royal University students, including beginners, regular gym users, and students looking for workout partners.

3 Testing Task

Participants will explore the website, create a demo profile, answer matching questions, review fictional sample matches, indicate interest in a workout partner, and complete the feedback survey.

Find a Buddy Pilot Feedback Survey

4 Evidence to Collect

Recorded automatically by the website (stored in each tester's browser only):

  • Feedback survey responses (interest in the pilot, comfort meeting new partners, comfort with sample matches, fitness goals, schedules, activities)
  • Demo match reactions (interested / not interested on sample profiles)

Recorded manually by researchers:

  • Whether each participant completed the matching task
  • Observed difficulties, questions, or confusion during testing
  • Verbal feedback and safety concerns raised

5 Observable Success Signal

Proposed success criterion (not an actual result): the test will be considered successful if at least 7 out of 10 students complete the matching process and indicate they would join the free Gym Buddy pilot.

We selected 7 out of 10 participants as our initial success threshold because it provides a practical indication of student interest before investing in further development. This is a proposed benchmark rather than a statistically validated threshold.

6 Decisions Based on Results

  • 7–10 successful testers: Proceed with a small voluntary pilot at MRU.
  • 4–6 successful testers: Improve matching questions or usability and test again.
  • 0–3 successful testers: Investigate barriers and reconsider the matching approach.
Please note: all student profiles shown on this site are fictional demonstrations. Gym Buddy is currently a classroom prototype, not a live service. Feedback survey responses are stored in each user's own browser (localStorage) and are not automatically collected from different devices. Researchers must export CSV files or record results manually to gather data across testers.
